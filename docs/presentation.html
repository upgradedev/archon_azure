⚡ ΕΦΚΑ · Law 4387/2016

EFKA (ΕΦΚΑ — Ενιαίος Φορέας Κοινωνικής Ασφάλισης) is Greece's unified social insurance fund, successor to IKA. Employer mandatory contribution: ~22% of gross salary — paid directly to EFKA as a separate institutional transfer, never appearing in the bank payroll line.
